OFFICIAL CLASSIFICATION

CR Consulting Policy Model

10 points · High Risk

Any CAD 10,000+ transaction, or a qualifying activity trigger combined with high-risk-country exposure.

5 points · Medium Risk

Two CAD 1,000+ transactions in five years, one CAD 3,000+ transaction, or high-risk-country exposure.

0 points · Low Risk

No five-point or ten-point policy trigger.

BEHAVIORAL MONITORING

Schema1 Index

Separate 0–100 monitoring indicator derived from transaction count, country and beneficiary diversity, 30/90/365-day volume, country risk and occupation risk. It supports review prioritization but does not replace the official policy tier.

Behavior Index = weighted Schema1 components ÷ 66 × 100
GOVERNANCE

Compliance Approval Guard

  • Protective escalations apply immediately.
  • Automated reductions cannot override a higher approved tier.
  • All rating changes remain subject to Compliance Officer approval and documented rationale.
  • The system blocks a risk-rating change when the reason or reviewer is missing.
  • Automated commentary supports, but never replaces, analyst judgment.
OPERATIONS

Risk-Based Review Cycles

  • KYC refresh: High 12 months, Medium 24 months, Low 36 months.
  • Annual EDD review: currently High-risk customers only.
  • Due dates remain anchored to the last completed review.
  • Shared-beneficiary alerts require 2+ distinct senders and the configured CAD threshold.
  • Business relationship trigger: 2× CAD 1,000+ or 1× CAD 3,000+ in five years.
PRIVACY

Local Processing

The workbook is processed inside the browser. This package has no server connection, login or cloud database. Risk movement history is stored locally in browser storage unless exported.

QUICK START

Upload the four-tab workbook

  1. Click Upload Excel.
  2. Select an XLSX or XLSM workbook containing Data, Country, Occupations and Schema1.
  3. The Data tab may use either the previous 58-column structure or the new 59-column structure containing Counterparty Number.
  4. Wait while the browser maps fields and calculates the portfolio.
  5. Use KYC Refresh Due for risk-based refreshes, High-Risk EDD Review for annual EDD, Shared Beneficiary Alerts for network patterns, and Business Relationships for qualifying customers.
  6. Download Updated Excel to retain Compliance Notes, Risk Rating History, KYC Refresh History and EDD Review History.

No information is sent outside the browser. Legacy XLS files must first be saved as XLSX.
